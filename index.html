From raw data to an explained recommendation

The same nine-phase pipeline as the project, condensed into five stops.

1

Build the graph

O*NET occupations, LinkedIn postings and Coursera courses become typed nodes and edges.

2

Weight every edge

Recent postings count more; skills confirmed by two sources earn higher confidence.

3

Learn embeddings

Node2Vec captures structure; a two-layer HGT refines it with relation-aware attention.

4

Map your skills

Your résumé is read in the browser and matched to skill nodes in the graph.

5

Rank and explain

Each career comes with matched skills, missing skills, graph paths and courses.

Built on three public datasets

O*NET 31.0

Occupations and the technologies they use, including “hot technology” flags that feed edge confidence.

LinkedIn job postings

Skill demand per occupation, weighted by recency. 20% of postings are held back to test the recommender on real profiles.

Coursera catalogue

Courses linked to the skills they teach, and to the occupations they prepare you for.
